## 📂 Projects & Impact

### 🟣 Roserva — Appointment Booking SaaS
Full-featured SaaS platform for managing appointments and reservations, built with NestJS, Vue.js, and PostgreSQL. Includes scheduling, client management, automated reminders, and a clean booking interface.

**Impact:** Enables businesses to digitize and streamline their appointment workflows, reducing no-shows and eliminating manual scheduling overhead.

---

### 🎓 Digital School Justification System
Fully automated the process of managing student absences and late arrivals, with digital justification generation and WhatsApp notifications.

**Impact:** Reduced paper usage, improved traceability, and direct communication with students.

---

### 🏛️ Inventory Management System for City Hall
Built a platform to track and manage the entire inventory of municipal assets including furniture, equipment, and tools.

**Impact:** Improved asset control, reduced losses, and clearer audit processes.

---

### 🖥️ Service Management System for Government Tech Office
Internal tool to handle technical service requests, maintenance, internet support, and computer equipment servicing in public offices.

**Impact:** Transparent service tracking, detailed history per device, faster response times, and better task organization.

---

### ⚙️ RESTful APIs and Microservices for Admin Applications
Designed and developed robust APIs using NestJS, Prisma, PostgreSQL, and Docker, with CI/CD for seamless deployments.

**Impact:** Scalable and maintainable architecture for internal and external systems.
